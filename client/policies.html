Returns & Refunds

Customer care and returns information for Effata Atelier purchases.

Our Returns Commitment

At Effata Atelier, we take pride in creating thoughtfully designed products with care and attention to detail. Customer satisfaction is important to us.

1. Change-of-Mind Returns

We encourage customers to carefully review their orders before completing payment. Change-of-mind returns may be considered within 7 days of receiving the product, subject to applicable statutory cancellation rights.

  • Items must be unused and in their original condition.
  • Original packaging and tags should be retained where applicable.
  • Proof of purchase must be provided.
  • The customer is responsible for eligible change-of-mind return courier costs.

2. Damaged or Faulty Products

If you receive a damaged, defective or incorrect product, please contact us as soon as reasonably possible with your order number, a description of the issue and clear photographs where applicable.

3. Custom and Made-to-Order Products

Products made specifically to a customer's requested design, specifications or personalisation are generally not eligible for change-of-mind returns.

4. Refunds

Approved refunds will be processed using the original payment method where possible. Processing times may depend on the payment provider or financial institution.

5. Exchanges

Exchanges may be considered depending on product availability. Please contact us before returning an item.

6. Return Shipping

For faulty, damaged or incorrectly supplied products, Effata Atelier covers reasonable return courier costs. For eligible change-of-mind returns, the customer is responsible for return courier costs.
